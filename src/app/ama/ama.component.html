FAQ

How quickly will I get an answer?

Typically within a few minutes if I'm available. If I'm busy or working (or sleeping), it might take a bit longer, but I always respond.

Is this really anonymous?

Yes, absolutely! No personal information is collected unless you choose to provide an email for notifications.

What kind of questions can I ask?

Any question at all! Personal, professional, technical, philosophical - I'm here for it all.

Will my question be public?

No. Each question is private unless you choose to share your unique session link.

How do I access my questions from another device?

Click "Copy Session Link" and open it on any device. Your questions are tied to your unique session ID, so you can access them from anywhere.
